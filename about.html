Our story

Why we started PureSweep

We're two friends who both believe a clean space makes for a better life — so we decided to build this cleaning company together. We wanted to offer more than tidying up: we wanted customers to feel they could hand this one thing over and stop worrying about it.

From the very first visit, we put detail and communication first — confirming what you need up front, giving a clear quote, showing up on time, and following up after every job.

What we stand for
  • Honest, transparent pricing
  • Respect for your time and space
  • Always learning better cleaning methods
  • Every job treated like the first one
